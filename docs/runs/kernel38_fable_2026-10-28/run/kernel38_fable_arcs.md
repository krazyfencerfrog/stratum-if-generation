# kernel38_fable: arcs and node expansion

State visibility: **observed**. You are a housekeeper, trained to be unnoticed and therefore to notice: where an arc stands shows in whether a lady rings for you or her own maid, whether the valet lifts his head when you pass, whether the Major says your surname to dispatch you or to keep you in the room. Nobody tells a servant what they think of her; she reads it off the linen and the stairs.
