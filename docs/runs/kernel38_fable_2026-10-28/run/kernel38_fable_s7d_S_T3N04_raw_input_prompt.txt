ONE scene for the engine. The engine is room-based: the player moves between
the rooms this scene opens, examines things, talks to people, and acts
through a menu (verb, then object, then detail). Everything the player can
DO in this scene beyond looking and talking is what you write: each action
is one menu entry with what happens when it is taken. Code adds the
mechanics (what each choice does to the story's states, which flags it
sets); you write the scene.

   sentences: where they are, what has changed, who is here, what presses.
   It tells the world, never a deed of the player's ("You carry the saddle
   into the square" is a choice the player did not make): the player acts

   them, and never left unsaid.
