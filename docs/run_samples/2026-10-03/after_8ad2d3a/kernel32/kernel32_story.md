# kernel32: story outline

Kernel: A dungeon crawl where your only companion is a cursed talking sword that wants to be put back where it was found. Funny, but with teeth.

## Story form: Fichtean curve + In medias res
The story starts with the protagonist already in the tower, carrying the curse, and escalates through a series of worsening crises—barred gates, rising water, grinding iron—driven by the sword's desire to return. It lands on the final confrontation at the surface door, where the choice between companionship and belonging is settled by the tower's rule, ending with a wry or bittersweet resolution.
Why this form: Its shape of climbing through distinct crises to a climax matches the 3b.primary_trajectory of escalating and the 3e.timeline_duration of hours_to_days, while its short aftermath fits the 3c.moral_valence of a quick, decisive restoration or loss rather than a long winding down.

The question: Do you put the cursed sword back where it belongs, or keep it as your companion and weapon?

## Lines
### T1: The Homebound Blade
- motivation: You start wanting to leave the bell tower alive with the cursed sword still in your hand. By the end you want to keep that voice and that edge even though it means carrying its homesickness out into the world as a permanent pull.
- strategy: Keep using the sword to cut and pry the tower's iron, trade only what the tower can physically hold, and defer the niche until the surface door is in front of you.
- turning point: On the flooded stair the sword cuts you over the gap and shouts for the niche, and the warden's hand on the upper gate makes clear that the door will cost either the key-iron or the sword's return; after that the only way out is to hand over the key-iron and keep the blade.
- ending (N05) Out With the Niche Still Empty: You leave the bell tower with the cursed sword still in your hand, the warden keeping the key-iron, and the reliquary niche below still empty. The sword's stated desire to be returned follows you out as a constant, annoying pull, and the tower's rule remains bent rather than satisfied.
- path: N01 > N02 > N03 > N04 > N05

### T2: The Empty Hand
- motivation: You start wanting to leave the bell tower alive with the cursed sword still in your hand, but as the water closes the stair you come to want the silence of empty hands and the end of the blade's homesick pull.
- strategy: Leave the sword where the tower can count it, climb with your own hands, and let the warden open the door on the tower's terms instead of bending the rule with the blade.
- turning point: At the flooded stair, the sword's shout at the niche and the rising water make the blade's company feel like a weight you can drop, and you choose the ledge over the hilt.
- leaves T1 after N03 when: you set the cursed talking sword down on the niche's ledge and climbed with your own hands
  - instead of: you let the cursed talking sword cut the iron pins and carry you over the flooded gap
  - why the shift: The water makes the sword's weight and shout unbearable, and the ledge is right there: you see that keeping the blade means carrying its homesickness out of the tower, while setting it down would end the noise and the pull.
- differs: T1 keeps the sword and bends the rule, so you leave with the blade's voice as a permanent companion; this line answers the question by putting the sword back, so you leave empty-handed and silent with the tower's rule satisfied.
- ending (T2N02) The Tower's Terms: You leave the bell tower with empty hands and no sword's voice; the niche below is full, the warden has turned the key-iron, and the tower's rule is satisfied. The price is the loss of the blade's aid and bond, leaving you to carry only what your own hands can hold.
- path: N01 > N02 > N03 > T2N01 > T2N02

### T3: The Counted Return
- motivation: You start wanting to leave the bell tower alive with the cursed sword still in your hand, but after the warden counts the set-down you come to want the tower to accept that count as the return, so you can keep the blade without carrying its homesick pull as a debt.
- strategy: Keep the sword in your hand, let the warden’s count stand, and make the surface door open on the tower’s terms by presenting the counted return rather than the actual niche.
- turning point: When the warden counts the sword as returned for the stair and the sword’s voice changes from wanting the niche to wanting to stay, you stop trying to defer the return and start trying to make the count good.
- leaves T1 after N02 when: you set the cursed talking sword in the niche’s ledge for the warden to see and then took it back
  - instead of: you cut the gate's pin with the cursed talking sword and let him hold the broken pin as proof the rule was bent
  - N02 must now contain: The lower stair gate must make the reliquary niche’s ledge reachable and visible to the warden, so the set-down and take-back can be counted at the gate.
  - why the shift: The warden marks the set-down on the tower’s count, and the sword’s voice stops asking for the niche and starts saying it wants to stay; that makes the return feel possible without losing the sword.
- differs: T1 keeps the sword and bends the rule, paying a permanent homesick pull; this line keeps the sword and satisfies the rule by a count, paying the sword’s voice as a borrowed thing that the tower can still claim. T2 returns the sword and empties your hands; this line keeps the voice and the blade while letting the tower call the return satisfied.
- ending (T3N03) Held by Count: You leave the bell tower with the cursed sword still in your hand, the warden having turned the key-iron on the strength of the counted return. The tower’s rule is satisfied, the niche below is empty, and the sword’s voice no longer pulls you home but reminds you that it is borrowed.
- path: N01 > N02 > T3N01 > T3N02 > T3N03

## Nodes
### N01: The Iron Tally [Opening crisis; turn 1, way 1]
lines: T1, T2, T3
where: the lower stair gate
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

You stand at the barred lower stair gate with the cursed talking sword in your grip, and the tower warden shows you an iron tally that names the tower's price. The tally says the surface door opens only if the empty reliquary niche is refilled or the key-iron is in the tower's hand. As he watches the sword, the first fitting in the gate grinds shut, registering the curse before you have passed it.

### N02: The Broken Pin [Second crisis; turn 2, way 2]
lines: T1, T2, T3
where: the lower stair gate, the spiral stair
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

The tower warden keeps the spiral stair barred while the cursed talking sword is in your grip, and you cut the gate's pin with the blade, letting him hold the broken iron as proof the rule has been bent. The stair opens, but the sword's stated desire to be returned grows louder in your hand. The next fitting on the stair is stiff and will not turn easily.

For T3, this node must also contain: The lower stair gate must make the reliquary niche’s ledge reachable and visible to the warden, so the set-down and take-back can be counted at the gate.

### N03: The Flooded Gap [Third crisis; turn 3, way 1]
lines: T1, T2
where: the spiral stair, the upper landing
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

The spiral stair is narrowing with water from the flooded crypt, and the iron fittings are grinding shut as the last way up closes. The cursed talking sword cuts the remaining pins and carries you over the flooded gap before the stair seals. At the upper landing, the sword's stated desire to be returned becomes a shout at the niche, and the tower warden's hand is already on the upper gate.

### N04: The Door's Price [Climax; turn 4, way 2]
lines: T1
where: the upper landing
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

The sealed surface door stands before you, the empty niche visible below, and the tower warden waits with the key-iron in his hand. You hand him the key-iron and cut the door's bolt with the cursed talking sword, so the door opens while the niche stays empty. The warden keeps the iron, and the sword's demand to be put back follows you out as a burden.

### N05: The Homebound Pull [Aftermath; ENDING]
lines: T1
where: outside the bell tower
who: the cursed talking sword

You stand outside the bell tower with the cursed sword still in your hand, the sealed door closed behind you, and the reliquary niche below still empty. The warden keeps the key-iron inside, and the tower's rule remains bent rather than satisfied. The sword's voice does not stop; it shifts from complaint to a constant homesick pull.

### T2N01: The Key-Iron Turns [Climax; turn 4, way 1]
lines: T2
where: the upper landing, the reliquary niche
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

The sealed surface door is in front of you, the reliquary niche is open below, and the tower warden stands between you and the key-iron. You set the cursed talking sword back in the niche, and the warden turns the key-iron so the surface door opens. You pass him with empty hands while the sword's voice stops at the threshold and the niche below holds the blade.

### T2N02: The Empty Hand [Aftermath; ENDING]
lines: T2
where: outside the bell tower
who: -

The sealed door is closed behind you, and the tower's rule is satisfied with the niche below full and the key-iron turned. You stand outside with no sword's voice in your head, your hands still checking for a hilt that is no longer there. The loss of the blade's aid and bond leaves you carrying only what your own hands can hold.

### T3N01: The Narrow Way Up [Third crisis; turn 3, way 3]
lines: T3
where: the spiral stair, the upper landing
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

The spiral stair is filling from the crypt and the iron fittings are closing in around you. You take the warden's key-iron and work it into the upper gate before the last way up seals, while the warden sets the tower's hazard running. The bolt grinds half shut, leaving only a narrow way to the upper landing, but the warden leaves the sword in your hand because the count from the ledge is still standing, and the sword's voice shifts from homesick to watchful.

### T3N02: The Counted Return [Climax; turn 4]
lines: T3
where: the upper landing
who: the tower warden, the cursed talking sword

At the sealed surface door the warden waits, and you hand him the key-iron and show him the count where the sword was set down and taken back. He turns the key-iron on the tower's terms, and the door opens while the sword remains in your grip. The empty niche below is not refilled, but the tower accepts the counted return.

### T3N03: Borrowed Blade [Aftermath; ENDING]
lines: T3
where: outside the bell tower
who: the cursed talking sword

You stand outside the bell tower with the sword still in your hand while the surface door closes behind you. The sword's voice is quieter now, no longer homesick but watchful, and it reminds you that the tower has counted it as returned without taking it back. The niche below is empty, and the blade stays with you as a borrowed thing.
