Second-person fantasy. You're the aging leader of a mercenary guild, training up a hot-headed successor before your body finally gives out. At some point you'll have to decide: step down and hand the guild to them for good, or hold onto command until you literally can't anymore. If you step down, I want the rest of the story to actually be played as them, picking up your unfinished business. If you never step down, it should stay your story straight through to the end.
