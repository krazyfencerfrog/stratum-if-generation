Second-person heist thriller. You're a thief breaking into a corporate vault. If you go in loud — cutting through walls, disabling guards by force — you never touch the building's security system directly, it's a straight physical job start to finish. If you go in quiet, you'll need to jack into the building's AI to slip past its defenses, and for those few minutes you're not fully yourself — you're seeing the vault through the AI's own sensors and thoughts, until you're back in your own head to finish the job on foot. Either approach should feel like a real, complete way to get through the vault.
