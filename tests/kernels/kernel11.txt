A second-person post-apocalyptic story. You lead a small band of survivors through a collapsing city. Two things matter equally to me and should both be central pillars of the story: whether survival justifies abandoning your principles (Survival vs. Principle), and whether the group should be run by one strong decisive leader or governed by consensus (Autocracy vs. Consensus). I don't want either of these treated as more important than the other.
