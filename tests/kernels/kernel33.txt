A slow-burn romance set over one summer at a failing seaside hotel. You're the new night manager; the other is the chef, who is about to leave for a better kitchen in the city. There's a rival for your attention and a reason the chef hasn't gone yet that nobody will say out loud. I want the pleasures of the genre: the near-misses, the one night everything almost happens, the moment one of you has to say it first. Several endings, and at least one where it works out.
