A ghost story that is funny and frightening in equal measure. You inherit a decrepit canal boat with a resident ghost who insists he is still the captain and will not let the boat leave its mooring. Your only living companion is your brother-in-law, who came to help you sell it and who wants his money back more than he wants you safe. I want the ghost to be a real character, scary when he needs to be, and I want the boat to actually go somewhere.
