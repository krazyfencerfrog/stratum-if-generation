An epic fantasy adventure. You're a young hedge-witch from a border village who gets swept up in a quest to stop an old dragon that has woken in the northern mountains. Along the way you gather a small party — a disgraced knight, a thief who knows the old roads, and a scholar from the capital who has her own reasons for wanting to reach the dragon first. I want real travel: forests, a river city, ruins, the mountain pass. The companions should matter, with their own loyalties that can pull against yours. different endings depending on how you deal with the dragon and who is still with you at the end.
