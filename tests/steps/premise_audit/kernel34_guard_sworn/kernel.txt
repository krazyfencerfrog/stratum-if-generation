Court intrigue. You're the tutor to a child king whose uncle, the regent, means to crown himself at the midsummer rite in nine days. The regent is clever, patient, and genuinely believes the boy is unfit. You have no soldiers: only what the child trusts you with, what the court's servants let slip, and whichever of three courtiers you can bring over. The regent's plan should advance on its own whether or not I do anything about it, and I want to be able to lose people I've won.
