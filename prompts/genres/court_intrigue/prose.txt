--- COURT INTRIGUE PROSE & STYLE GUIDELINES ---
1. LINGUISTIC PRECISION & SUBTEXT:
   - Dialogue must operate on two levels: the formal, elegant court etiquette spoken aloud, and the lethal political daggers moving beneath it.
   - Use physical micro-expressions: the slight tightening of a jaw, the hesitation of a signet ring being turned on a finger, the quick glance at the guards by the arched doorway.
2. SENSORY ATMOSPHERE:
   - Oppressive splendor; beauty laced with anxiety; the sensation of walking a tightrope over the heads of smiling executioners.
