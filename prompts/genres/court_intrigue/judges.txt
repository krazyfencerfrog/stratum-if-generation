--- COURT INTRIGUE SEMANTIC AUDIT GUIDELINES ---
1. VERIFY POLITICAL & PSYCHOLOGICAL STAKES: Confirm that obstacles, leverage, and turns revolve around political maneuvering, royal legitimacy, courtier loyalties, and personal secrets—never bureaucratic financial paper or crude hack-and-slash brawling.
2. VERIFY AUTONOMOUS THREAT: Confirm that the regent/rival actively pursues their goal and represents a genuine, capable danger.
3. VERIFY WARD / CHILD AGENCY: Confirm that the child king/ward is an active dramatic presence whose trust matters, not a static prop.
