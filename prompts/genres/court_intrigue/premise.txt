--- COURT INTRIGUE PREMISE & DRAMATIC ENGINE GUIDELINES ---
1. OPPOSITION ARCHETYPE:
   - The primary opposition is a patient, respected regent or veteran minister who wields legitimate state machinery, commands the guards, and genuinely believes the rightful heir is unfit to rule.
   - Secondary opposition comes from ambitious neutral courtiers, mercenary councilors, and court informants playing all sides.
2. PRICE & FAILURE DISCIPLINE:
   - The price of failure is total and personal: execution for treason, public branding, a poisoned cup, or watching the rightful heir imprisoned in a tower.
   - The price of victory is moral compromise: betraying an innocent servant to protect a secret, trading royal favors to corrupt nobles, or hardening a child's heart against his own kin.
3. MEDIATION LEVERS:
   - Levers are sovereign trust, hidden royal seals, servant gossip, compromising love letters or private vices, ceremonial protocols, and promises of high office.
