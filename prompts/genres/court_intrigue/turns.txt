--- COURT INTRIGUE TURNS & DILEMMAS GUIDELINES ---
1. PROGRESSION & PACING:
   - Turn 1 (The Looming Deadline): Establish the rival's deadline (e.g. nine days to coronation) and the protagonist's meager starting leverage (e.g. the child's private trust, one loyal servant).
   - Turn 2 (Testing the Waters): Approaching key courtiers or investigating the regent's inner circle; uncovering a critical vulnerability or dirty secret.
   - Turn 3 (The Counter-Stroke): The regent strikes back—arresting an ally, tightening palace curfews, or attempting to poison or discredit the protagonist.
   - Turn 4 (The Rite / Climax): The day of the ceremony arrives. Playing the final hand before the crown is set: confronting the regent before the assembled peers, executing a palace coup, or turning the regent's own allies against him.
2. DILEMMA TAXONOMY:
   - Dilemmas test loyalty vs. survival, protecting the child's innocence vs. teaching him ruthlessness, and trusting a courtier vs. keeping total secrecy.
3. DYNAMIC COALITIONS:
   - Ways through must reflect the shifting status of the three key courtiers—which one was won over, which was compromised, and which defected.
