--- COURT INTRIGUE READER PROMISES GUIDELINES ---
1. AUDIENCE FANTASY: Outmatching ruthless, experienced powerbrokers from a position of apparent vulnerability; manipulating high-stakes royal politics through intellect, psychological insight, and razor-sharp timing.
2. OBLIGATORY SET-PIECES:
   - The Stolen Audience: A hurried, dangerous whisper in a torch-lit alcove, private gallery, or curtained bedchamber where one false word brings treason.
   - The Public Feast or Council: A formal court gathering where mortal political battle is waged beneath rigid etiquette, loaded toasts, and polite smiles.
   - The Pawn's Pivot: A moment where a courtier or servant shifts allegiance under pressure, revealing the fragile foundation of the protagonist's coalition.
   - The Showdown at the Threshold: The eve of the coronation or ritual where the final gambit is played—exposing the regent's treason, securing the boy king's authority, or fleeing into exile.
3. TANGIBLE OPPOSITION: The rival or regent must be formidable, patient, and articulate—presenting a coherent, seductive justification for their power that challenges the protagonist's convictions.
