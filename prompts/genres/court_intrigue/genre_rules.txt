--- COURT INTRIGUE CRAFT RULES ---
1. THE TICKING CLOCK & AUTONOMOUS OPPOSITION: Power does not wait for the protagonist. The regent, usurper, or rival faction has an active timeline (e.g. an approaching coronation, festival, or royal rite) that advances relentlessly turn by turn unless directly disrupted or counterbalanced.
2. ASYMMETRIC WEAPONS & DENIABILITY: Without military force or physical armies, power is exercised through asymmetric levers: whispered confidences, servant corridors, compromising secrets, intercepted gifts, personal loyalties, and targeted public disgrace.
3. TRANSACTIONAL LOYALTY & SHIFTING ALLIANCES: Courtiers are not permanent allies; they are self-interested actors with distinct personal ambitions, family rivalries, and secret vices. Every ally won can be alienated, bought out, or intimidated away if the player fails to protect their interests.
4. ZERO BUREAUCRATIC BOREDOM: Stakes are sovereign power, royal bloodlines, liberty, and the scaffold. Never bog down dramatic confrontation in administrative tax petitions, commercial receipts, or dry ledger audits. Drama lives in the smile before the poisoned chalice, the public glance of contempt, and the sovereign word.
