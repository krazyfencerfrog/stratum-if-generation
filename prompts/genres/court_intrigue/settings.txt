--- COURT INTRIGUE SETTINGS GUIDELINES ---
1. ARCHITECTURAL DUALITY:
   - Contrast gilded formal halls (marble thrones, sunlit throne rooms, echoing galleries where every whisper carries) with the shadowed service underbelly (narrow spiral stairs, servant sculleries, damp wine vaults, curtained confessionals).
2. SENSORY GROUNDING:
   - The rustle of heavy velvet and silk; the scent of burning beeswax, sweet almond oil, and stale wine; the cold draft slipping under heavy tapestries; the heavy ring of iron halberds striking flagstones when guards change watch.
