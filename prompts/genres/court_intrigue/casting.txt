--- COURT INTRIGUE CASTING GUIDELINES ---
1. CORE DRAMATIS PERSONAE:
   - The Child / Vulnerable Ward: Innocent, perceptive, emotionally dependent on the protagonist, possessing royal blood but lacking political armor.
   - The Regent / Usurper: Patient, dignified, intellectually formidable; never a cartoon villain, but a statesman who sees his usurpation as necessary for the realm's survival.
   - The Three Courtiers:
     - The Proud Commander: Values honor and martial strength; swayed by legitimacy and strength of will.
     - The Hedonistic Diplomat / Treasurer: Values wealth and self-preservation; swayed by blackmail or guaranteed future privileges.
     - The Pious Prelate / Scholar: Values divine order or legal tradition; swayed by ancient oaths or evidence of sacrilege.
   - The Palace Servant / Whisperer: Invisible, observant, moving through secret corridors; loyalty bought with gold or genuine kindness.
2. BREAKING POINT DISCIPLINE:
   - Courtiers break and abandon the protagonist when exposed to immediate treason charges without protection, when publicly humiliated, or when convinced the regent's victory is inevitable.
