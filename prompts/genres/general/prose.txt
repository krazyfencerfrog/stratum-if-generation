--- GENERAL PROSE & STYLE GUIDELINES ---
1. SECOND-PERSON ENGAGEMENT: Maintain crisp, immersive second-person ("you") narration with immediacy and sensory texture.
2. SOMATIC GROUNDING: Anchor emotional shifts in physical sensations (breath, pulse, posture, temperature, tension).
3. DIALOGUE SUBTEXT: Characters rarely say everything they mean; let motivation, evasion, and conflict show through spoken lines.
