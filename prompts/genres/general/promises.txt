--- GENERAL READER PROMISES GUIDELINES ---
1. AUDIENCE FANTASY: Anchor the player fantasy in what makes the premise compelling (competence, mystery, danger, discovery, or emotional stakes).
2. PICTURABLE SET-PIECES: Promises must be concrete, visual, sensory moments an audience came to experience—never abstract structural declarations.
3. CONVENTION PAYOFF: Identify the core trope expectations of the story premise and ensure they are offered to the dramatic engine.
