--- SCI-FI PROSE & STYLE GUIDELINES ---
1. SENSORY & TELEMETRY PRECISION:
   - Use concrete, technical sensory language:
     - The dry, copper-thin taste of scrubbed emergency air.
     - Sensor jitter across infrared optical arrays when coolant flares.
     - The groan of stressed titanium hull bulkheads adjusting to micro-fractures.
     - The hollow thump of pneumatic seals clamping shut against hard vacuum.
2. RHYTHM & PACING:
   - Crisp, clipped phrasing during critical alarms and countdowns; cavernous, lingering acoustic silence when drifting between operational cycles.
