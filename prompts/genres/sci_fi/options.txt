--- SCI-FI SCENE OPTIONS & AGENCY GUIDELINES ---
1. COMMAND & TRIAGE MODES:
   - Player options should embody distinct operational philosophies:
     - Pure Utilitarian Efficiency: execute cold calculations, vent compromised compartments, enforce power quotas regardless of emotional protest.
     - Empathetic Compromise: accept structural risk, jury-rig dangerous bypasses, share life-support deficits equally across sectors.
     - Direct Physical Intervention: dispatch drones, manual valve actuation, risk machinery to prevent loss of life.
     - Transparency & Truth: broadcast unvarnished telemetry to the crew, risking panic to earn honest cooperation.
2. SENSORY & SYSTEMIC IMPACT:
   - Choices immediately alter ship status: pressure dropping in bay 4, temperature rising in the reactor coil, drone telemetry going dark.
