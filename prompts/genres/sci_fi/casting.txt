--- SCI-FI CASTING GUIDELINES ---
1. FACTION & CREW ARCHETYPES:
   - Chief Engineer / Maintenance Lead: Pragmatic, sleep-deprived, protective of their machinery and technical crew, hostile to remote automated commands.
   - Civilian / Sector Representative: Speaks for the vulnerable families or agricultural tiers, desperate for life support, suspicious of cold command calculations.
   - Security Officer or Medical Officer: Enforces triage quotas or struggles with psychological casualties of long-term isolation.
2. BREAKING POINT DISCIPLINE:
   - Human crew break when automated commands withhold vital life support without warning, seal off living sectors without evacuation time, or treat living humans as expendable cargo.
   - Automated/AI companions break when commanded to violate core preservation directives or when sensory relays are severed.
3. EMBODIMENT & INTERACTION:
   - If the protagonist is an AI, interactions happen through acoustic speakers, maintenance drones, terminal displays, and security monitors, emphasizing the gulf between machine omniscience and physical helplessness.
