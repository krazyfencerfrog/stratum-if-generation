--- ROMANCE SCENE OPTIONS & AGENCY GUIDELINES ---
1. EMOTIONAL STANCES:
   - Player choices should embody distinct relational attitudes:
     - Leaning in: stepping into the other person's space, holding the gaze, asking the vulnerable question.
     - Playful banter: deflecting or probing with teasing subtext.
     - Guarded retreat: stepping back, hiding behind professional duty or formal politeness.
     - Direct confession: laying all defenses down and saying what is felt.
2. TANGIBLE STAKES:
   - Options risk emotional vulnerability, pride, and romantic clarity—not game-over screens.
