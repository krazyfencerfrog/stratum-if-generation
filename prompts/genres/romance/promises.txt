--- ROMANCE READER PROMISES GUIDELINES ---
1. AUDIENCE FANTASY: The electric tension of mutual attraction held back by circumstance, duty, or pride; the longing to be truly seen, chosen, and cherished.
2. OBLIGATORY SET-PIECES:
   - The Near-Miss: An intimate moment where two people almost kiss, almost touch, or almost speak the truth, abruptly interrupted by external duty or sudden self-consciousness.
   - The Enforced Proximity: A situation where the two are isolated together after hours (late-night kitchen prep, rain-sheltered porch, stranded together during a storm).
   - The Moment One Says It First: The terrifying, courageous leap of vulnerability where one person speaks the feelings out loud before knowing if they are reciprocated.
   - The Public Glances: Subtle, charged exchanges of eye contact in a room full of other people.
3. ROMANTIC RIVALRY: A tangible rival who actively competes for affection, attention, or social standing, creating romantic tension and jealousy rather than corporate recruitment.
