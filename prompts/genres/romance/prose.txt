--- ROMANCE PROSE & STYLE GUIDELINES ---
1. SOMATIC CHANNELS:
   - Prioritize somatic markers of intimate attraction and tension:
     - Gaze: holding eye contact across a counter, looking away when caught, watching eyelashes or mouth.
     - Breath: breath catching, exhaling softly against cold air, shallow breathing in close quarters.
     - Touch: fingertips hesitating, accidentally grazing hands while reaching for a dish, heat radiating through fabric.
     - Involuntary reactions: tightness in the throat, pulse fluttering against the collarbone, involuntary stillness.
2. DIALOGUE WITH SUBTEXT:
   - Dialogue should crackle with unspoken desire. Characters speak about recipes, guest reservations, or the summer fog when they are really speaking about whether the other person is going to leave.
