--- ROMANCE SETTINGS & ATMOSPHERE GUIDELINES ---
1. INTIMATE SPATIAL TEXTURE:
   - Romance thrives in spaces of enforced intimacy and quiet sanctuary: quiet kitchens after service, rain-sheltered verandas, sea-facing balconies at 3 AM, dimly lit pantries, narrow corridors where two people must turn their shoulders to pass.
2. SENSORY GROUNDING:
   - Emphasize acoustic intimacy (the sound of rain on glass, simmering broth, quiet sighs, the click of a latch, whispers that carry across an empty room).
   - Use lighting and temperature contrasts: warm candlelight against cold seaside fog, shared warmth of a single lantern.
