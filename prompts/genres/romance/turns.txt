--- ROMANCE TURNS & DILEMMAS GUIDELINES ---
1. PROGRESSION & PACING:
   - Early turns establish professional friction and lingering glances while working side-by-side.
   - Middle turns force isolation and near-misses (e.g. storm closing the doors, late night kitchen shift) where romantic feelings almost surface, interrupted by rival advances or sudden defensive withdrawal.
   - Penultimate turns force a crisis of jealousy, misunderstanding, or looming departure (e.g. rival makes a bold move, or the deadline to leave arrives).
2. DILEMMA TAXONOMY:
   - Dilemmas must be framed around vulnerability vs. pride/safety, speaking up vs. silence, and choosing shared passion vs. safe autonomy.
3. MANDATED RESOLUTION & FRUITION:
   - The final turn MUST include at least one pathway that cleanly delivers reciprocal romantic fruition (confessing feelings, asking them to stay, choosing each other).
   - The cost of fruition must be an emotional sacrifice (giving up pride, embracing the risk of love, declining an outside luxury)—NEVER a punitive outcome like getting fired, abandoned, or separated!
