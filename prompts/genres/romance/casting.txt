--- ROMANCE CASTING GUIDELINES ---
1. COMPANION / LOVE INTEREST DYNAMICS:
   - The love interest is deeply drawn to the protagonist but holds back due to a specific emotional defense (fear of unreciprocated feelings, career duties, past disappointment, or pride).
   - They express care through subtle deeds (saving the best portion, lingering after shift, remembering a small preference) rather than overt speeches.
2. BREAKING POINT DISCIPLINE:
   - CRITICAL: A companion's breaking_point in romance is triggered by EMOTIONAL COWARDICE, DECEIT, PUBLIC DENIAL OF FEELINGS, OR CHOOSING THE RIVAL.
   - A breaking_point MUST NEVER trigger on the protagonist confessing feelings, asking them to stay, speaking the core truth, or offering intimacy!
3. THE RIVAL:
   - The rival is a competing romantic interest or suitor who actively flirts with the protagonist or commands social charm to provoke jealousy.
   - Never cast the rival as a corporate recruiter with an employment contract or a company executive.
