--- ROMANCE SEMANTIC AUDIT GUIDELINES ---
1. VERIFY NON-PUNITIVE STAKES: Confirm the price and dilemmas are emotional/relational. Do not fail stories for lacking violent or lethal consequences.
2. VERIFY FRUITION PATH: Confirm that at least one ending path delivers mutual romantic fulfillment and that confession does not trigger abandonment.
3. VERIFY COMPANION BREAKING POINT: Confirm the love interest's breaking point is tied to silence, deceit, or cowardice, not to the protagonist expressing love.
