--- ROMANCE CRAFT RULES ---
1. PRIMARY AFFECT & CONFLICT: The emotional heart of romance is the tension between yearning for intimate connection and the fear of emotional vulnerability. The stakes are relational, emotional, and existential for the heart—not physical violence or corporate bureaucracy.
2. NON-PUNITIVE DRAMATIC PRICE: Romance prices are paid in emotional currency: pride surrendered, defensive walls dismantled, the risk of rejection endured, or safe autonomy sacrificed for mutual commitment. The price must never be punitive misery, death, or arbitrary cruelty.
3. MANDATED EMOTIONAL FRUITION: When a story premise promises romantic payoff, closure, or a relationship bearing fruit ("二人の関係が実を結ぶ"), the pipeline MUST guarantee at least one viable pathway where emotional courage is rewarded with mutual connection, reciprocal confession, and choosing each other.
