--- ROMANCE PREMISE & DRAMATIC ENGINE GUIDELINES ---
1. OPPOSITION ARCHETYPE:
   - The primary opposition is NEVER an impersonal corporate scout, bureaucratic inspector, or lethal monster.
   - The opposition must be a romantic rival (a competing suitor who charms or courts to provoke jealousy), conflicting duties/career ambitions, or personal defense mechanisms born from past heartbreak.
   - If the Kernel specifies a rival for attention (e.g. "あなたの気を引こうとするライバル"), the rival must actively court, flirt with, or charm the protagonist to spark jealousy and force the love interest to act.
2. PRICE & FAILURE DISCIPLINE:
   - Price in romance is non-punitive emotional risk: swallowing one's pride, confessing vulnerable feelings, risking heartache, or sacrificing safe professional detachment.
   - It is NEVER physical violence, termination of employment, or arbitrary ruin.
3. MEDIATION LEVERS:
   - The levers of romance drama are shared responsibilities, moments of physical closeness, unspoken confidences, and opportunities to either speak the truth or hide behind polite formality.
