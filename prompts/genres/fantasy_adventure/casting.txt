--- FANTASY ADVENTURE CASTING GUIDELINES ---
1. PARTY COMPANION ARCHETYPES:
   - Each companion represents a distinct worldview, martial/magical tradition, and cultural code (e.g. the disgraced knight seeking redemption, the street-smart guide who knows the old ways, the scholarly seeker with a hidden private agenda).
   - Companions hold critical knowledge, keys, or combat capabilities the protagonist lacks.
2. BREAKING POINT DISCIPLINE:
   - A companion's breaking_point is triggered when the party's actions violate their core sacred code:
     - Disgraced knight: leaves or turns if you slaughter defenseless captives or break a knightly oath.
     - Rogue guide: flees if the odds turn suicidal without a plan, or if cheated of agreed share.
     - Scholar: breaks if rare ancient knowledge or an essential artifact is destroyed out of blind superstition.
3. THE BEAST / ANTAGONIST:
   - The ancient dragon or antagonist speaks with terrifying intelligence, age, and alien perspective—not a mindless beast.
