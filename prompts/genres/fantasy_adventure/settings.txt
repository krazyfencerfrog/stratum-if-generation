--- FANTASY ADVENTURE SETTINGS GUIDELINES ---
1. GEOGRAPHIC EVOCATION & SCALE:
   - Convey the tactile reality of the elements: damp forest floor carpeted in pine needles, spray of roaring river rapids, freezing sleet scouring scree-covered slopes, heat shimmering above soot-blackened caldera stones.
2. ANCIENT RESIDUE:
   - Emphasize ruins that feel older than human memory: moss-eaten runestones, half-submerged arches of lost civilizations, rusted giant iron links anchoring broken bridges.
