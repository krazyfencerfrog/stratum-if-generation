--- FANTASY ADVENTURE PREMISE & DRAMATIC ENGINE GUIDELINES ---
1. OPPOSITION ARCHETYPE:
   - The primary opposition is an ancient mythic force (an awakened dragon, a corrupting planar rift, an unbound titan) whose scale dwarfs individual mortals.
   - Secondary opposition may include rival adventuring factions, fanatical cultists, or harsh environmental forces (a relentless winter closing the pass).
2. STAKES & PRICE:
   - Stakes are existential for regions, lineages, or ancient pacts.
   - The price must be tangible and irreversible: an enchanted artifact consumed, an ancient sanctuary collapsed to seal the beast, an eye lost to forbidden sight, or a loyal companion buried on the mountain.
3. MEDIATION LEVERS:
   - Levers are magical reagents, ancient runes, forgotten bypass tunnels, dragon-forged chains, and conflicting companion pledges.
