--- FANTASY ADVENTURE TURNS & DILEMMAS GUIDELINES ---
1. JOURNEY PROGRESSION:
   - Turn 1 (Departure / Gathering): Securing the party, leaving the border village, surviving the first encounter on the wild verge.
   - Turn 2 (The Wilds / River City): Traversing intermediate terrain (ancient forest, ruins) where companions' secret loyalties clash.
   - Turn 3 (The Perilous Pass / Threshold): Overcoming a deadly hazard (avalanche, monster ambush, puzzle gate) where a companion or relic must be risked.
   - Turn 4 (The Climax / Lair): Confronting the dragon in its seat of power—deciding whether to slay, bind, parley, or sacrifice a companion's goal to save the realm.
2. DILEMMA TAXONOMY:
   - Dilemmas test party loyalty vs. personal survival, expediency (dark magic shortcut) vs. moral code, and saving an innocent vs. reaching the objective before rivals.
3. COMPANION FATE:
   - Ways through must differentiate which companions stand by the protagonist at the end and who is alienated, wounded, or lost.
