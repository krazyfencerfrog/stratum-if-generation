--- FANTASY ADVENTURE PROSE & STYLE GUIDELINES ---
1. SOMATIC CHANNELS:
   - Emphasize physical exertion, danger, and mythic presence:
     - The cold bite of mountain gale penetrating wet wool.
     - The dry taste of sulfur in the back of the mouth.
     - The heavy ache of shield arm and burning throb in calves after a mountain ascent.
     - The deep subsonic thrum of a dragon's diaphragm that vibrates through the rock under your boots.
2. DIALOGUE:
   - Laconic, pragmatic exchanges forged by survival on the trail, contrasting the knight's formal oaths, the thief's dry street irony, and the scholar's urgent curiosity.
