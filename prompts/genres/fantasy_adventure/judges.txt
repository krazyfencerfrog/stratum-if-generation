--- FANTASY ADVENTURE SEMANTIC AUDIT GUIDELINES ---
1. VERIFY PARTY DYNAMICS: Confirm that companions are active dramatic agents whose distinct loyalties and codes matter to the narrative.
2. VERIFY JOURNEY & SETTING DIVERSITY: Confirm that the story moves through distinct physical settings across its turns and does not collapse into a single room.
3. VERIFY MYTHIC PAYOFF: Confirm that the dragon or ancient threat is confronted directly and that resolution costs are meaningful.
