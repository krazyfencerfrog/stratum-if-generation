--- FANTASY ADVENTURE READER PROMISES GUIDELINES ---
1. AUDIENCE FANTASY: Gathering a brave and fractious band, walking into the uncharted wild, unearthing wonders of a fallen age, and standing before a legendary beast with only wits, companion bonds, and raw courage.
2. OBLIGATORY SET-PIECES:
   - Campfire on the Road: Guard shifts, mending boots, tense arguments over maps, and quiet moments sharing lore under unfamiliar stars.
   - The Ruined Threshold: Traversing sunken river cities, decaying watchtowers, or cyclopean gates that warn mortals away.
   - The Natural or Magical Hazard: Navigating a howling mountain blizzard, crumbling rope bridge, or shifting magical ward that tests the party's endurance.
   - Confronting the Mythic Beast: Meeting the awakened dragon face-to-face in its lair—smoke, ancient intelligence, and lethal majesty.
