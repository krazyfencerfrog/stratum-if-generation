--- FANTASY ADVENTURE CRAFT RULES ---
1. PRIMARY AFFECT: Wonder, mythic peril, the call to adventure, and hard-won camaraderie against ancient forces.
2. EXPEDITION & TRAVEL DYNAMICS: The journey itself is dramatic—each region (frontier forest, ruined river city, treacherous pass, draconic lair) brings physical hardship, shifting terrain, and cultural frontiers.
3. PARTY COHESION & DIVIDED LOYALTIES: Companions are not passive hirelings; they possess conflicting personal oaths, cultural taboos, and hidden stakes that pull against party unity when pressure mounts.
4. IRREVERSIBLE SACRIFICE: Victory or survival in epic fantasy demands tangible sacrifice: ancestral steel broken, ancient relics spent, blood prices paid, or companions choosing to hold the rear.
