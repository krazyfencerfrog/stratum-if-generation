--- FANTASY ADVENTURE SCENE OPTIONS & AGENCY GUIDELINES ---
1. TACTICAL & MORAL AGENCY:
   - Options must offer contrasting adventure philosophies:
     - Direct martial courage: draw steel, stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the knight.
     - Rogue cunning: search for a blind spot, slip through shadows, disable the trigger.
     - Hedge-magic intuition: commune with earth, cast a charm, read the subtle disturbance in the air.
     - Diplomatic parley: address the creature with reverence, appeal to ancient treaties.
2. COMPANION ALIGNMENT:
   - Choices demonstrate which companion's counsel you honor and which you override.
