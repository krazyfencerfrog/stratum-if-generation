--- GHOST STORY READER PROMISES GUIDELINES ---
1. AUDIENCE FANTASY: Navigating an uneasy, atmospheric co-existence with the dead; uncovering the poignant, eerie truth behind a historic tragedy; mastering an unruly haunted vessel or haunted inheritance through wit, grit, and respect.
2. OBLIGATORY SET-PIECES:
   - The Manifestation: The terrifying first moment the ghost proves it is not a hallucination, swaying the deck, cutting the engine, or speaking with the chill of deep water.
   - The Domestic Squabble with the Dead: An exasperating, darkly funny confrontation over shipboard rules, kitchen cupboards, or navigation charts where the ghost asserts its lingering authority.
   - The Cold Peril: A life-threatening physical crisis (e.g. entering a narrow canal lock in a storm, an engine fire, or being caught in treacherous currents) where the supernatural forces the living to their limits.
   - The Uncovering of the Anchor: Learning why the spirit remains chained to this vessel—a sunken cargo, a drowned crewmate, an unadmitted failure of command, or a broken promise.
3. LIVING COMPANION TENSION: The mortal companion (e.g. an impatient relative, greedy creditor, or anxious skeptic) provides interpersonal friction, demanding the asset be sold or abandoned while the protagonist is trapped between the living and the dead.
