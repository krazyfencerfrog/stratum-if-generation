--- GHOST STORY PREMISE & DRAMATIC ENGINE GUIDELINES ---
1. OPPOSITION ARCHETYPE:
   - The primary opposition is the ghost itself: an obstinate, powerful spectral authority who controls the vessel's physical behavior (snapping lines, freezing throttles, slamming doors) to enforce its ancient command.
   - Secondary opposition comes from living companions: greedy relatives, debt collectors, or local waterway authorities who want to scrap, seize, or burn the boat.
2. PRICE & FAILURE DISCIPLINE:
   - The price of failure is being pulled into the deep, succumbing to freezing hypothermia, having the boat shattered against a weir, or losing sanity and inheritance to the haunting.
   - The price of peace is emotional recognition: honoring the dead captain's achievements, completing their final voyage, or surrendering personal profit to lay the spirit to rest.
3. MEDIATION LEVERS:
   - Levers are nautical tools, diesel engine repairs, the captain's logbook, personal relics left in the cabin, mooring cleats, compass bearings, and respecting naval protocol.
