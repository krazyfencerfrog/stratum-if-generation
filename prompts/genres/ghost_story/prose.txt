--- GHOST STORY PROSE & STYLE GUIDELINES ---
1. ATMOSPHERIC SHIFTS:
   - Seamlessly transition between comedic, earthy domestic friction (bickering over a damp kettle) and the sudden, breath-stopping invasion of supernatural dread (temperatures plummeting, water pouring from dry ceilings, the spectral face appearing in dark glass).
2. SENSORY DETAILS:
   - The smell of river silt and ancient pipe tobacco.
   - The hair on the back of the neck lifting before a door handle turns by itself.
   - The freezing chill that radiates from the captain's silhouette.
