--- GHOST STORY CRAFT RULES ---
1. SPECTRAL ANCHOR & UNFINISHED OBSESSION: A ghost is not a generic magical monster; they are an indelible human soul bound to a physical vessel, place, or relic by an unresolved earthly fixation, tragic grudge, or unyielding professional pride (e.g. a captain who refuses to admit he is dead and will not let his craft leave port).
2. BALANCING DREAD AND EERIE HUMOR: Honor both the chill of genuine supernatural terror and the dark, dry comedy of cohabitating with an obstinate apparition. The ghost can be exasperating, petty, and eccentric one moment, and terrifyingly cold, uncanny, and lethal the next.
3. STRICT BAN ON GENERIC CLICHÉS:
   - NEVER introduce arbitrary "water salute ceremonies", generic incense-burning banishment rituals, salt circles, or psychic ghost-whisperer jargon.
   - The supernatural operates through tangible physical constraints: rusted machinery, cold drafts, locked hatches, mooring ropes that refuse to slip, water that freezes in the pipes, and direct spoken confrontation.
4. FORWARD MOMENTUM & VESSEL MOVEMENT: The story must move in physical space! If the premise involves a haunted boat, vehicle, or journey, the craft must not remain stuck at the dock all story. Navigating locks, canals, rapids, or misty waterways is essential to the narrative arc.
