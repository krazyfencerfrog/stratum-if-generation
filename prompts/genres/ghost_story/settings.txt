--- GHOST STORY SETTINGS GUIDELINES ---
1. NAUTICAL & WATERWAY TEXTURE:
   - The smells of damp bilge water, stagnant canal duckweed, rusted iron cleats, diesel exhaust, and wet wool coats.
   - The sound of black water lapping rhythmically against the hull; floorboards groaning under invisible footsteps; chains clinking in the dark.
2. WEATHER & CONFINEMENT:
   - Clammy river mist rising at dusk; driving autumn rain hammering on the tin roof of the galley; the suffocating confinement of the narrow boat cabin illuminated by a single kerosene lantern.
