--- GHOST STORY CASTING GUIDELINES ---
1. CORE DRAMATIS PERSONAE:
   - The Ghost Captain: Gruff, proud, Victorian/Edwardian nautical speech, fiercely attached to his vessel. Capable of eerie supernatural manifestations (frost on windows, ropes moving on their own, voice rumbling from the bilge) but also vulnerable to pride and old naval protocol.
   - The Mortal Companion (e.g. Brother-in-Law): Intensely pragmatic, anxious about money, hates the damp, terrified of the ghost yet desperate to sell or scrap the boat for cash.
   - The Canal Keeper / Waterfront Local: Knows the dark folklore of the water, warns about the black locks, and hints at the historic tragedy that took the captain's life.
2. BREAKING POINT DISCIPLINE:
   - The ghost violently lashes out if the living attempt to tow the boat to a scrapyard, insult his seamanship, or disrespect his crew's memory.
   - The mortal companion breaks and flees into the rain if supernatural terror escalates beyond their greed.
