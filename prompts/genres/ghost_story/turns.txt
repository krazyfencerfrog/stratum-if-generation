--- GHOST STORY TURNS & DILEMMAS GUIDELINES ---
1. PROGRESSION & PACING:
   - Turn 1 (The Mooring / First Contact): Inheriting the decrepit boat; the ghost asserts control and locks down the mooring; the living companion demands a quick sale.
   - Turn 2 (Casting Off / The First Mile): Bypassing the ghost's initial blockade through mechanical cunning or honoring his rank; the boat actually moves onto the canal or waterway.
   - Turn 3 (The Perilous Hazard / Narrow Lock): A narrow canal lock, midnight fog, or approaching weir forces living and dead into frantic, terrified cooperation or violent spectral resistance.
   - Turn 4 (The Final Mooring / Reckoning): Reaching the historic site of the captain's demise or destination; choosing whether to banish, lay to rest, or reach a lasting maritime pact with the spirit.
2. DILEMMA TAXONOMY:
   - Dilemmas test siding with the living companion's financial greed vs. the dead spirit's honor; choosing safe surrender at the dock vs. braving haunted waters.
3. PHYSICAL VESSEL ADVANCEMENT:
   - Each turn must physically advance the boat along the waterway, through changing canal scenery, industrial brick basins, and lonely marshland stretches.
